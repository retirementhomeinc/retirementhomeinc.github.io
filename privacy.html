Privacy Policy — Arjun health

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What the app accesses. The "Arjun health" application requests read-only access to your Google Health and Fitbit data across three scopes: activity & fitness, health metrics & measurements, and sleep. It does not write or modify any of your data.

How data is used. Data is fetched only when you explicitly request it, processed locally on your own machine, and is not stored or shared with any third party beyond Google's APIs.

Revoking access. You can revoke the app's access at any time via your Google Account → Security → "Third-party access", or by removing this app's consent in your Google Account.
